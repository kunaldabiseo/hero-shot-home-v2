08 The Zero to Hero Method

How a shoot actually runs

Almost everybody says they hate having their photo taken. The coaching is why our clients end up liking the result. It is a five-step method we run in every session, whether it is one person or two hundred.

ZeroHero
  1. 01STEP 01

    Your goals

    Where the image will be used, what it needs to say, and who needs to believe it.

  2. 02STEP 02

    Coach & connect

    A few minutes of conversation before the camera comes up. This is what stops the rabbit-in-headlights look.

  3. 03STEP 03

    Build confidence

    We show you the first frames on screen so you can see it is going well, rather than hoping.

  4. 04STEP 04

    Find your pose

    A position that is natural for you specifically, not a template applied to everyone.

  5. 05STEP 05

    Shoot, review, improve

    Shoot, look, adjust, repeat. You leave knowing what you are getting.

If you are working to a hard deadline (a website launch, an annual report, a conference), tell us the date in your enquiry and we will say yes or no straight away rather than leaving you waiting.

For company bookings we invoice, and we are happy to work with a purchase order and your standard terms. A deposit secures the date for larger rollouts; for a straightforward team day the invoice usually follows the shoot.
